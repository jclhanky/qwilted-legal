Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Qwilted (“Qwilted,” “we,” “us”) is a personal app for saving and organizing short-form videos, links, and notes, operated by Jacob Hanks. This policy explains what information Qwilted handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Qwilted, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Who can use Qwilted

Qwilted is intended for people 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. If you are between 13 and the age of legal majority where you live, please review this policy with a parent or guardian.

2. Information Qwilted handles

Account information

You sign in with Sign in with Apple. We receive a unique Apple identifier and, only if you choose to share it, your name and email address (you can use Apple’s “Hide My Email” to keep your address private). This is used to create and secure your account.

Content you save

The links, titles or captions, personal notes, collection names, importance ratings, and timestamps you create are stored locally on your device.

Content sent for AI organization

When you save an item or use the Ask / auto-sort features while online, Qwilted sends the item’s URL, the source app name, the caption or title, any text extracted from the page, your note, and the names of your collections to our backend. The backend uses a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a summary, tags, and collection suggestions, which are returned to you. If you don’t use AI features, or you’re offline, this content is not sent.

Photos

If you choose a cover photo for a collection, Qwilted accesses the specific photo you select from your library, and stores it locally for that collection. Qwilted does not otherwise access your photo library.

Subscriptions

Purchases of Qwilted Plus are processed by Apple. We receive your subscription status from Apple; we never receive your full payment card details. Qwilted keeps a local count of your monthly AI usage to enforce free and paid limits.

Diagnostics & tracking

No third-party tracking Qwilted contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites. If you have enabled sharing diagnostics with developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, non-identifying crash and usage data through the App Store.

3. How information is used

  • To provide the app — saving, organizing, and searching your content.
  • To generate AI summaries, tags, and collection suggestions when you use those features.
  • To manage your subscription and enforce free-tier limits.
  • To secure accounts and respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Service providers we share with

We share information only as needed for the following functions:

  • Apple — sign-in and payment processing.
  • Render — cloud hosting for the backend that processes save and AI requests.
  • Anthropic — AI processing of the content you choose to organize, to generate the response returned to you.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. AI processing

Content you send for organization is transmitted over an encrypted connection and processed to generate a result. It is not used to build advertising profiles about you.

6. Storage and retention

  • Most of your saved content lives on your device and is removed when you delete items or your account. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted for 7 days, then are permanently removed.
  • Content sent for AI is processed to return a result.
  • Account and subscription records are retained as needed to operate the service and meet legal obligations.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete: Settings → Delete Account removes your account and its data. You can also delete individual items at any time.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or California’s CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. We do not sell or “share” personal information as defined by California law.
  • To make a request, contact us at the address below.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International users

Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will revise the “Last updated” date above. Material changes may also be highlighted in the app.
