It looks only at messages you write, and it's meant only for plain statements, not hints, jokes, song lyrics or quotes. It reads on your phone and sends and keeps nothing.   • It can be wrong both ways. It can miss something serious, and it can now and then show up when nothing is wrong. Don't rely on it to notice that someone needs help.
  • What to do. If you or someone else is in danger right now, call 911 in the US or Canada, 000 in Australia, or your local emergency number. In the US or Canada, call or text 988, any time.
